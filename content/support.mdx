### When is Bristle available?

Bristle is coming soon to iPhone. Join the waitlist on the [home page](/) and we'll send one email when it's ready.

### Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos are sorted on-device. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to make.

### What if I delete something by mistake?

Nothing is deleted until you confirm it. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, so you can restore them from the Photos app.
